This policy works alongside our [Child Safety Standards](/child-safety), which apply to everything on Oguaa.

## Scope

This policy applies to mentorship, training and youth-facing opportunity listings on Oguaa. It covers those listings, how they are moderated, and the minimum information they must include before publication.

## No direct matching chats on the platform

Oguaa does not offer private messaging between mentors and young people, anonymous messages, or one-click pairing. Listings are public programme notices that send people to the organiser's own official channel.

## Safeguards for every programme

- Every mentorship listing must link to a publicly available safeguarding policy. We reject listings without one when they are submitted.
- A programme that involves anyone under 18 must require a parent or guardian's consent before they take part.

## Guardian consent

- If a mentorship programme includes children, the listing must say that guardian consent is required and explain clearly how the organiser collects it.
- The organiser collects and keeps the consent records. Oguaa does not store children's identity documents.

## Moderation and enforcement

- Curators may hold, reject or unpublish a mentorship listing that leaves out safeguarding controls, suggests unsafe ways of contacting young people, or appears to get around the guardian consent requirement.
- Repeated or high-risk breaches can lead to account suspension and, where required, a referral to the relevant authorities.
- To raise a concern about a youth programme, use Report on the listing and choose **Child safety**, or see how to reach us in the [Child Safety Standards](/child-safety).
