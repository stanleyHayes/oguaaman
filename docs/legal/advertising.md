## How ads are chosen

- **Ads are contextual.** An ad is chosen only by the page and section it appears on, such as the home page or the news pages. It is never chosen by who you are.
- **No profiling or tracking.** We don't use your profile, location, reading history, political opinions or any other personal data to choose ads, and we don't use advertising IDs or cross-site trackers. Requests for ads never carry your account.
- **Fixed places.** Ads appear only in set places on a page, such as a banner on the home page, a card in a list of stories or a box beside or below an article. An ad is never placed inside the text of an article.
- **No ads in your inbox.** Ads are never sent to members by email, WhatsApp or push notification.
- **No moving ads.** We don't accept animated or video ads, pop-ups or ads that cover the page.

Every ad has an ⓘ button that explains why you are seeing it and lets you report it.

## How ads are labelled

- Every ad carries a clear label saying it is an ad, and is styled so that it can't be mistaken for news.
- A commercial ad shows "Sponsored ·" followed by the advertiser's name. A public-service ad from a government body shows the agency's name in the same way.
- A political ad is labelled "Political ad" and shows "Paid for by" followed by the sponsor's verified legal name.
- An ad that contains AI-generated or digitally altered images is labelled "Contains AI-generated or altered media".
- An ad is not an endorsement by Oguaa of the advertiser, its products or its views.

## Prices

- **One public rate card.** Every ad is priced per 1,000 viewable impressions. An impression is viewable when at least half of the ad is on screen for one continuous second while the page is open in front of you.
- **The same rates for everyone.** Each placement has one commercial rate and one political rate. The political rate is the same for every party, candidate and campaign. We don't give discounts, coupons or private prices to anyone.
- **Full price shown.** Prices are in Ghana cedis (GH₵). Every quote shows the price before tax, the tax and the full price you pay.
- **Price changes.** A new rate applies only to new quotes. Once you submit a campaign its price is fixed, and a later change to the rate card doesn't affect it or any campaign you have paid for. Every change to the rate card is recorded with its reason.
- **No overselling.** We sell only the impressions we expect to deliver, so a placement can be full for the dates you want. The booking form tells you the most that is available.

## Review before you pay

- Our staff review every ad, its sponsor and its landing page **before you pay**. You pay nothing for an ad we reject.
- If we reject an ad, we tell you why. You can fix it and submit it again.
- When we approve an ad, the approval is valid for **72 hours**. Pay within that time to book the campaign. After that, the approval expires and the space is released.
- We check every sponsor before their first ad runs. Campaigns start no sooner than the lead time shown when you book, so that there is time for review.

## What ads may contain

### Every ad must

- be truthful, and make no claim you can't back up;
- not be disguised as news: no "Breaking", "News flash", "Just in" or similar wording, and no imitation of Oguaa's news pages;
- contain no hate speech, and nothing that stirs up ethnic, tribal or religious hostility;
- state any price in Ghana cedis only;
- link to a landing page that matches the ad and is served securely (https);
- declare any AI-generated or digitally altered images or video when you book, so that we can label the ad;
- have a short text description of its image, for people who use screen readers.

### Not accepted

- tobacco, cigarettes and vapes;
- crypto-currency and foreign-exchange trading;
- adult and sexual content;
- weapons;
- "spiritual" money-doubling, miracle cures and similar offers;
- infant formula;
- male-vitality and sexual-performance products.

Alcohol and gambling ads are not accepted at present, because Oguaa's readers may include people under 18.

### Regulated products

- **Food, drinks, medicines, herbal products and cosmetics** need the product's Food and Drugs Authority (FDA) registration and the FDA's approval of the ad. The approval must be valid for the whole campaign, and the ad can't claim that a product cures or treats a disease.
- **Financial services** need a licence from the Securities and Exchange Commission, the Bank of Ghana or the National Insurance Commission, which we check against the regulator's public register. Ads can't promise guaranteed returns.

## Political and election ads

Political ads are ads by or about a party, a candidate or a campaign, and ads about a political issue. They follow every rule above, and these as well:

- **Verified sponsors.** We verify the sponsor's identity and contact details before any political ad runs. A sponsor must be a Ghanaian citizen or, for an organisation, at least 75% Ghanaian-owned, as the Political Parties Act, 2000 (Act 574) s.24 requires, and must declare this when registering.
- **No anonymous sponsors.** Names such as "Concerned Citizens", "Friends of…" or "Well-wishers" are not accepted. The "Paid for by" line always shows the sponsor's real legal name.
- **Issues, not insults.** Political ads must be about policies and issues.
- **No false statements.** Ads must not make false statements about a candidate's character, or claim falsely that a candidate has withdrawn (Representation of the People Law, 1992 (PNDCL 284) s.39).
- **No early results.** Ads must not claim or project results before the Electoral Commission declares them.
- **No misleading voting information.** Ads must not give wrong information about when, where or how to vote.
- **Election blackout.** No political ad runs from 00:00 on the day before a poll until the blackout set for that election ends. Election ads must end before the blackout starts, and issue ads can't be booked during one. Any political ad still running when a blackout begins is stopped, and the undelivered share is refunded.
- **District Assembly elections.** District Assembly and unit committee elections are non-partisan under Article 248 of the Constitution. We accept an ad for a District Assembly candidate only with the Electoral Commission's written authorisation for it, and only once we have chosen to accept these ads. Such an ad may not show a party name, symbol or colours.
- **Two-person review.** Each political ad is approved by two members of our staff, or by one senior administrator.
- **Public Ad Library.** Every political ad that is booked to run is listed in Oguaa's public Ad Library with the sponsor's legal name, the party, candidate and constituency where they apply, the election, the dates, the number of impressions delivered and the exact amount paid and refunded. If we remove a political ad, it stays in the library with the reason. Entries are kept for 7 years after the ad ends. The library also lists every ad running now, with the advertiser's name as it appears on the ad.
- **Invoices.** Invoices are issued in the sponsor's name.

## Delivery, refunds and cancellation

- **Cancel before the start date:** you get a full refund.
- **Stopped after the start, removed by us, or not fully delivered:** if you stop a campaign after it starts, if we remove it, or if it ends without delivering all the impressions you bought, you get a refund of the undelivered share of the full price you paid.
- **Paid after the approval expired:** if your payment arrives after the approval has expired, or after the campaign was cancelled or rejected, you get a full refund.
- **Paused campaigns:** if we pause a campaign, its dates don't move. Anything not delivered by its end date is refunded.
- **How refunds are paid:** to the card or Mobile Money account you paid with, through Paystack. We don't deduct Paystack's fee from your refund. Amounts under GH₵1 are not refunded. How long a refund takes to reach you depends on your bank or Mobile Money provider.
- **Your cancellation right:** when you book, you ask for your campaign to start on its start date. Under the Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772) s.49(4)(d), your 7-day right to cancel ends once the campaign starts at your request. Until then you can cancel for a full refund.

## Reporting an ad and takedown

- Every ad has a "Report this ad" option. Our staff review every report.
- You can also send a notice about an ad, for example a copyright or legal complaint, to [hello@oguaaman.com](mailto:hello@oguaaman.com), as the Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772) s.94 provides.
- We may pause or remove an ad at any time if it breaks this policy or the law. We record the reason, tell the advertiser and refund the undelivered share.
