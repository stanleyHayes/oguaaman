This notice covers the Oguaa website at oguaaman.com, the web app at citizen.oguaaman.com, the creator studio, the staff console and the Oguaa mobile app for iPhone and Android. It is written to meet Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and the privacy rules of the Apple App Store and Google Play. The same text is published on the web and in the app.

## Who we are and how to reach us

Oguaa is an independent community platform for Cape Coast (Oguaa), in the Central Region of Ghana, and its people at home and abroad. Oguaa decides why and how your personal data is used, so it is the data controller for the processing described here.

- **Data-rights requests:** use the form at [citizen.oguaaman.com/privacy/request](/privacy/request). You don't need an account. You get a reference number, and we reply within 40 days (21 days for an objection).
- **Email:** [hello@oguaaman.com](mailto:hello@oguaaman.com). Put "Privacy" in the subject line.
- **Deleting your account:** see [citizen.oguaaman.com/account/delete](/account/delete).

## The short version

- You must be 18 or older to have an account.
- Your email address and phone number are never shown on your public profile, and other members can't look them up.
- Your password is stored only as a one-way hash. We never see it.
- We don't sell your data, we don't show advertising, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.
- Some things you post are public by design: your profile, your listings, tributes, reviews and notices. Some safety notices are published straight away and checked afterwards.
- Payments are handled by payment providers. We never see or store your card number or Mobile Money PIN.
- You can download a copy of your data or delete your account at any time.

## What we collect and why

For each kind of data we say where it comes from, what we use it for, and the legal basis under Act 843: your **consent**, a **contract** with you (providing the service you asked for), a **legal obligation**, or our **legitimate interest** in running a safe community platform. Items marked "required" are needed for that feature to work. Without them you can't use it, but you can still use the rest of Oguaa.

### Your account

- **Email address or phone number** (required to join). Used to sign you in, send sign-in, verification and password-reset codes, and send the account, safety and transaction messages you need. Basis: contract.
- **Password** (required). Stored only as a salted bcrypt hash, which cannot be turned back into your password. Basis: contract.
- **Date of birth** (required to join). Checked once when you sign up to confirm you are 18 or older. **We do not store it.** We keep only the date your age was confirmed. Basis: legal obligation and legitimate interest (keeping minors off the platform).
- **Your agreement to these terms:** which versions of the Terms of Use and this notice you accepted, when, and on which app. Basis: legal obligation.
- **Two-factor sign-in, if you turn it on:** your authenticator secret, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and your backup codes, stored as bcrypt hashes. Basis: contract.
- **Security records:** failed sign-in counts (used to lock an account under attack), when you last changed your password or two-factor settings, and your active sign-in sessions. Basis: legitimate interest (account security).

### Your profile

Everything here is optional, and you choose what to add: display name (required to join), photo, bio, quarter or town, Asafo company, schools, web links, the city and country you live in if you live abroad, and a birthday as a month and day only (never a year). Your birthday is shown to others only if you switch on "broadcast my birthday". Your profile is shown publicly because that is the point of a community directory. Basis: contract and consent.

### What you post

Listings (businesses, events, places, projects, campaigns, properties, opportunities, memorials and more), photos, tributes, candles, reviews, news articles, safety incident reports and lost & found notices. These are public once published. Basis: contract.

- **Photos** you upload through our own server have their hidden location and camera details (EXIF metadata) removed before they are stored.
- **Safety incident reports** include the category, a description, where it happened and how to reach you. Your contact details are never shown publicly. Only you and the safety staff (curators, stewards and moderators) can see them.
- **Lost & found notices** include a description, the last-seen place, an optional photo and, for a missing person, the guardian details and police reference you give. People reply through Oguaa's message relay, so your contact details are never published.
- **Crime and medical reports, notices about missing children, and any post our automated screen holds** are checked by a curator before anyone else can see them. Other safety and lost & found notices are published straight away because they are time-critical, and staff review them afterwards.

### Payments

When you buy a ticket, make a pledge or donation, subscribe a business, promote a listing, place a shop order or fund an Oguaa Outside job, we keep: the amount, what it was for, the payment reference and status, the date, and the email address the receipt goes to. For a shop order we also keep the buyer's name, email, phone number, delivery address and note, because the seller needs them to fulfil the order. Basis: contract and legal obligation (tax and accounting records).

Card and Mobile Money details go straight to the payment provider (Paystack, Stripe or Apple). Oguaa never receives or stores your card number, security code or Mobile Money PIN.

### Selling on Oguaa and business verification

To take payments in a shop, a business owner gives us: the business's legal name, registration number and tax identification number (TIN), the owner's Ghana Card number, identity documents, a settlement bank or Mobile Money account, a GhanaPost GPS address and a business contact email or phone. Identity documents are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM and can be opened only by you and our vetting staff, never through a public link. Buyers see the seller's legal name, location, business contact details, registration number and verification date on product pages, as consumer law requires. Basis: contract and legal obligation.

### Oguaa Outside agents

To be vetted as an agent you give us: an identity document (stored encrypted, as above), a guarantor's name and phone number, your payout Mobile Money or bank details, and the services and areas you cover. We keep your jobs, escrow payments, disputes and reviews. Tell your guarantor that you are sharing their details with us. Basis: contract and legitimate interest (protecting clients).

### Bookings, affiliates and institutions

- **Artist booking requests:** your name, contact details and event details are shared with the artist you ask to book.
- **Affiliate programmes:** your affiliate code, payout details and commission records.
- **Institution roles:** the schools, assemblies or organisations you manage or work for on Oguaa, and what you did in that role.

### Messages and notifications

- **In-app notifications**, and your choices about which kinds you receive.
- **Push notifications:** if you turn them on, a push token for your browser or device.
- **Email and WhatsApp:** we use your email address or phone number to deliver the messages you have chosen.

Safety alerts, account messages and transaction messages are always sent, through the channels you have switched on. Community and remembrance notices are on until you switch them off. News about Oguaa itself ("product" messages) is off until you switch it on. You can change all of this in your notification settings. Every email also lets you unsubscribe: use your email app's unsubscribe button, or the link at the bottom of the email and then confirm. Basis: contract and consent.

### The writing assistant

The writing assistant is optional and works only after you agree to its terms. It sends the text you choose to Anthropic (Claude), a company in the United States, to write a suggestion. Before the text leaves Oguaa, email addresses, phone numbers and Ghana Card numbers are replaced with placeholders, so the provider doesn't see them. Don't include other private details. We keep a daily count of how often you used the assistant, but not the text itself. The assistant writes suggestions only and never makes decisions about you. You can withdraw your consent at any time. Basis: consent.

### Reports, blocks and moderation

When you report or block someone, we keep the report or block. When someone reports content, we keep a snapshot of it as evidence. We also keep the moderation decisions staff make and the results of our automated content screen, which can hold a post for a person to review. Basis: legitimate interest (a safe community) and legal obligation.

### Technical data

- **IP address:** used to limit repeated requests (for example, too many sign-in attempts), and recorded in security logs for sign-ins and staff actions.
- **Listing views:** to count unique views, we record your member ID (or, if you aren't signed in, your IP address) once per listing per day, and delete the record after 30 days.
- **Location:** only when you choose "use my location" on a map. On the explore map, your position goes from your device to the routing service to draw walking directions. On a listing form, the place you pick is saved with the listing.

Basis: legitimate interest (security, preventing abuse, basic statistics) and, for location, your choice to share it.

### Privacy requests

When you make a data-rights request, we keep your name, contact details, the request and our handling of it. Basis: legal obligation.

## Who can see your data

- **Everyone:** your public profile and everything published that you posted.
- **Other members:** the people you deal with see what the transaction needs. A seller sees a buyer's order details until 30 days after the order is fulfilled. An artist sees your booking request. The person who posted a lost & found notice receives your relayed message.
- **Oguaa staff:** curators, stewards, moderators, editors and vetting officers see what they need for their role. Staff accounts must use two-factor sign-in, and every staff look at an identity document is recorded.
- **Service providers:** the companies below process data for us.
- **Authorities:** a court, the police or a regulator when the law requires it, or when a child or someone else is at risk (see our [Child Safety Standards](/child-safety)).

We never sell personal data and never share it for advertising.

## Service providers and transfers abroad

These providers process personal data for us, only to deliver their service:

- **Paystack** (card and Mobile Money payments): your receipt email, the amount and a reference. Only when you pay or are paid.
- **Stripe** (card payments in the mobile app): your card details and the amount. Only when you use it.
- **Apple** (in-app purchases on iPhone): the purchase record. Only when you use it.
- **Anthropic** (the writing assistant, United States): the redacted text you send. Only when you use it.
- **Moonshot AI** (Kimi, a backup writing assistant, China): would receive the same redacted text, but only if the main assistant fails and the backup is switched on. It is **switched off** on the live service. We will update this notice before switching it on.
- **Resend** (email delivery): your email address and the message.
- **Meta** (WhatsApp Business, message delivery): your phone number and the message. Only when you receive WhatsApp messages.
- **Expo, Google, Apple and Mozilla push services** (push notification delivery): a device or browser push token and the alert. Only when you turn on notifications.
- **Cloudinary** (image hosting): the images you upload, when image hosting is switched on.
- **MongoDB Atlas** (database hosting): all platform data.
- **Render** (API hosting, Frankfurt, Germany): all platform data in transit, plus server logs.
- **Vercel** (website hosting): your IP address and the pages you request.
- **OpenStreetMap** (map tiles and place search) **and the OSRM routing service** (walking directions): your IP address, the map area or place you search for and, for directions, your position. Your device sends these directly when you use a map.
- **Google Fonts** (web fonts on the websites) **and unpkg** (the map library in the mobile app): your IP address when the page loads.
- **Google Maps:** only if you tap "Directions" or "Open in Google Maps", which opens Google's own service.

Our servers are in Germany, and several providers are in the United States or other countries. Your data therefore leaves Ghana. We send each provider only what it needs for its task. We use providers that publish data-protection and security commitments. Traffic to and from Oguaa is encrypted (HTTPS). If we add a provider or change what one receives, we update this notice first.

## How long we keep it

- **Account and profile:** until you delete your account.
- **Things you post:** until you or staff remove them, or you delete your account (see below).
- **Sign-in, verification and password-reset codes:** 10 minutes. Two-factor sign-in challenges: 5 minutes. Account-deletion codes: 15 minutes.
- **Sign-in sessions:** 30 days, or until you sign out on that device, change or reset your password, or turn two-factor sign-in on or off.
- **In-app notifications:** 12 months.
- **Writing-assistant usage counts:** 90 days.
- **Listing-view records:** 30 days.
- **A buyer's contact details, as seen by the seller:** until 30 days after the order is fulfilled. After that the seller sees only an initial.
- **Identity and verification documents:** until you delete your account, or earlier if you ask. We delete them when your account is deleted.
- **Payment records** (tickets, pledges, subscriptions, promotions, orders, escrow jobs): kept after you delete your account for tax, accounting and payment-dispute purposes, with your name, email, phone number and address removed. App Store purchase records are kept for 6 years, without your identity.
- **Reports, moderation, audit and privacy-request records:** kept without your name, so we can show how the platform was run and how your request was handled.
- **Server logs:** kept briefly by our hosting provider for security and fault-finding.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from your account settings on the web or in the app. If you can't sign in, request deletion at [citizen.oguaaman.com/account/delete](/account/delete) and confirm with a code we send you.

When you delete your account, we delete your profile, contact details, password, uploaded files, identity documents, blocks, follows, notifications, push registrations and usage counts. Photos and videos you uploaded that still appear on content that stays up (an institution's page, a published article or another member's page) stay with that content, no longer linked to you. Records we must keep are moved off your account id onto a new random one. Your listings are taken down and their contact details removed, except listings you posted for an institution, which stay with it. Reviews, tributes and news articles you wrote stay up under "Former member", with your name removed. The records listed under "How long we keep it" are kept without your identity. If you are a party to a funded escrow job or have unfulfilled paid orders, you need to settle those first.

## Your rights

Under Act 843 you have the right to:

- **Be informed:** that is what this notice is for.
- **Access your data:** download a full copy, including this processing information, from your account settings, or ask through [the request form](/privacy/request).
- **Correct your data:** edit your profile at any time, or ask us to correct anything else.
- **Delete your data:** delete your account yourself, or ask us to delete specific data. The records we must keep are listed above.
- **Object to processing:** including to processing based on our legitimate interest. Objections are answered within 21 days.
- **Withdraw your consent:** at any time. Switch off the writing assistant, birthday broadcasts or notification categories in settings. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything done before.
- **Stop marketing:** we don't send marketing unless you switch on product news, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
- **Not be subject to decisions made only by a computer:** automated checks such as rate limits and our content screen can refuse a request or hold a post, but a person reviews anything held, and no decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you by automated means alone.
- **Complain to the Data Protection Commission:** if you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission. We would welcome the chance to put things right first.

People without an Oguaa account, for example someone named in a memorial or a news article, can use the same [request form](/privacy/request).

## How we protect your data

- All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), and the websites send strict security headers.
- Passwords and backup codes are stored as bcrypt hashes. Two-factor secrets and identity documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
- Staff access depends on role, staff accounts must use two-factor sign-in, and staff access to sensitive records is logged.
- Sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and an account locks after repeated failures.
- Changing or resetting your password, or turning two-factor sign-in on or off, signs out every other session. Signing out ends the session on the device you sign out on.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

## Children

Oguaa is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children, and we delete an account that belongs to a child as soon as we find out. Some notices are about children, for example a missing-child notice posted by a parent, guardian or relative, or by a teacher or official acting with the family's knowledge. These are checked by a curator before publication and taken down when the child is marked as found. See our [Child Safety Standards](/child-safety) and [Safeguarding Policy](/safeguarding).
