These terms apply when you buy from a shop, buy an event ticket, make a pledge or donation, book an artist or pay an Oguaa Outside agent through Oguaa. They add to our [Terms of Use](/terms). They don't take away any rights you have under Ghanaian consumer protection law.

## Who you are buying from

Oguaa is the platform. The business, organiser, artist or agent is the seller and is responsible for what they sell. Before a shop can take payments, we verify its legal identity. Each product page shows the seller's legal name, location, business contact details and, where it has one, its registration number. Oguaa itself sells only its own services, such as business plans and listing promotions.

## Prices and payment

- Prices are shown in Ghana cedis (GH₵) and include everything you pay to the seller, apart from any delivery charge the seller shows you.
- You pay through Paystack (card or Mobile Money), or through Stripe (card, in the mobile app). Your card and Mobile Money details go directly to them. Oguaa never sees or stores them.
- Oguaa's platform fee (currently 5%) comes out of the seller's share. It doesn't increase the price you pay.
- A payment counts only when the payment provider confirms it. If a payment fails, no order, ticket or pledge is created.

## Order confirmation and receipts

When your payment is confirmed, we show a confirmation with a reference number, and the payment provider emails a receipt to the address you gave. Keep the reference number: you'll need it if anything goes wrong. If you were signed in when you paid, your shop orders and tickets also appear in your account.

## Shop orders

- **Delivery or pickup:** you choose at checkout. The seller prepares the order and tells you when it is ready or on its way.
- **Cancelling:** you can cancel an order before the seller dispatches it or marks it ready for pickup. Ask the seller, or contact us with your order reference. You get a full refund.
- **Goods not delivered, or not as described:** if your order doesn't arrive, or what arrives is faulty, damaged or clearly different from its description, contact the seller first. If the seller doesn't put it right, contact us within 14 days of the date the order should have arrived. You are entitled to a replacement, a repair or a full refund. If you return the goods, return them in the condition you received them.
- **Change of mind:** unless the seller's own policy says otherwise, you can't return goods just because you changed your mind. You can still cancel before dispatch.
- **Services** (for example a booked appointment): if the service isn't provided, or isn't provided as agreed, you are entitled to have it provided properly or to get your money back.
- Shops can't sell digital downloads or online content through Oguaa.

## Event tickets

- Your ticket is issued when your payment is confirmed. Show its code at the door.
- If a ticket tier sells out while your payment is going through, no ticket is issued and your payment is refunded.
- **If an event is cancelled,** you get a full refund of the ticket price. If an event is moved to a new date, you can keep your ticket or ask for a refund before the new date.
- Otherwise, the organiser's own refund policy applies. It is shown on the event page before you buy.

## Pledges and donations

Oguaa keeps a platform fee of 5% of each confirmed pledge. The rest goes to the project or campaign. The fee and the amount the project receives are shown before you pay. Pledges and donations can't be refunded once confirmed, unless the campaign is cancelled or the payment was taken in error. A campaign stops taking pledges after its deadline.

## Oguaa Outside jobs

The client agrees a quote with a vetted agent and pays it into escrow. The money is released to the agent when the client confirms the job is done. If there is a dispute, a vetting officer reviews both sides and decides whether to release the money to the agent or refund it to the client.

## Business plans and promotions

- Business plans and listing promotions are Oguaa's own services. They aren't sold in the Oguaa mobile apps.
- A plan lasts for the period you pay for. It doesn't renew automatically: to keep it, pay again before it ends, and the new period is added on. To cancel, simply don't renew.
- A promotion runs for the number of days you buy.
- Because these services start straight away, they can't be refunded once they begin, unless they weren't delivered as described.

## Disputes

1. Contact the seller, organiser or agent first. Most problems are solved this way.
2. If that doesn't work, contact us at [hello@oguaaman.com](mailto:hello@oguaaman.com) with your payment reference and what went wrong. We aim to reply within 14 days. Where the seller is at fault, we can require them to put it right, and we can arrange a refund through the payment provider.
3. You can also use the dispute process of your card issuer or Mobile Money provider, or go to the consumer protection authorities or the courts of Ghana.

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana.
