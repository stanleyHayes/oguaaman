This policy is part of our [Terms of Use](/terms). It applies to everything you post, upload or send on Oguaa, including listings, photos, profiles, tributes, reviews, news, reports and messages sent through the relay. We have zero tolerance for content and behaviour that breaks it.

## Be real and be local

- Post genuine people, places, businesses, events and stories. Don't invent businesses, projects or people.
- Don't pretend to be another person, a business, a traditional authority or a public institution.
- Don't post a death, crime or emergency that didn't happen, or a notice you know is false.
- Only claim a title or office that is really yours.

## Be respectful

- No harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation, whether against members, staff or anyone else.
- No hate: no attacks on people for their ethnicity, tribe, nationality, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or origin.
- Treat memorials and grieving families with care. No mocking the dead or their families.
- Don't encourage violence, self-harm or dangerous activities.

## Keep people safe

- Anything that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child is never allowed. We remove it, close the account and report it to the authorities. See our [Child Safety Standards](/child-safety).
- No sexually explicit content, and never share intimate images of someone without their consent.
- Don't share other people's private information: home addresses, phone numbers, ID numbers, health details or photos of them in private settings.
- Only a parent, guardian or close relative, or a teacher or official acting with the family's knowledge, may post a notice about a missing child. Include the police reference where there is one.

## Be honest in money matters

- No scams, fake fundraising, pyramid schemes or misleading offers.
- Only sell physical goods and real-world services that you are allowed to sell. You can't sell digital downloads, weapons, drugs, counterfeit goods or anything illegal through Oguaa.
- Describe what you sell accurately, and honour the prices and terms you show.
- Don't ask people to pay outside Oguaa to avoid its protections, such as escrow for Oguaa Outside jobs.

## Don't abuse the platform

- No spam, repeated posts or automated scraping, and don't try to get around limits, blocks or suspensions.
- Don't try to reach accounts or data that aren't yours, or to disrupt the service.
- Don't upload malware, and don't use the writing assistant to produce content this policy forbids.
- Don't use Oguaa to settle contested chieftaincy, title or leadership disputes. We hold such claims and refer them to the recognised traditional and civic authorities.

## Reporting and what happens next

Use the Report option on any listing, profile, review, tribute, product, news article, agent or writing-assistant suggestion. Our moderators review every report within 24 hours. Child-safety and intimate-image reports hide the content immediately. You can also block any member from their profile.

When content breaks this policy, we remove it. Depending on how serious it is, we also warn the person, suspend their account, or close it permanently. We report illegal content to the police or other authorities where the law requires, or where someone is at risk.
